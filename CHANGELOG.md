Strudel Samples changelog

2026-11-10, v2026.11.10. Added a word puller that grabs every short clip of one word. The first pull was the word hello, trimmed tight with misses dropped and a hello supercut added. Every sample is now named by its words, show, episode, and clip id, and a catalog lists them all.

Added short spoken clips and a puller script that fetches them, with a run log and a fallback when the first host fails. Candidate clips were sorted, trimmed, and joined into the final set.
